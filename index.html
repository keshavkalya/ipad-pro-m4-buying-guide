The iPadOS Limitation

Even with desktop-class M4 silicon, iPadOS still blocks macOS AU/VST audio plugins in Logic Pro (AUv3 only) and desktop FxPlug extensions in Final Cut Pro. On iPad, Logic Pro and Final Cut Pro are also tied to Apple Creator Studio at $12.99/month or $129/year rather than standalone Mac purchases.
